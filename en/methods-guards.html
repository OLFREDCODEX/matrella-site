Methods Guards AI

Guardians for Uncertainty Assessment in Research and Data Quality Systems

The MG AI project has concluded

Methods Guards AI explored how artificial intelligence could support methodological work in social research.

The prototype tested whether AI could help with an initial review of research documents by surfacing weaknesses, strengths and questions worth pursuing. Researchers and reviewers retained responsibility for methodological judgments and conclusions.

The project is closed. PDF uploads and online analyses have been disabled.
